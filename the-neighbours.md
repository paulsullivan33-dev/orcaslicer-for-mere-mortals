# The Neighbours

**Outcome:** where OrcaSlicer sits among
the slicers a mere mortal will meet,
so the choice between them is a choice,
not an accident.

**Check:** each neighbour is described
by its engine, its home turf, and the
reason someone picks it over OrcaSlicer —
or doesn't.

> None of the neighbours were run
> in this guide's lab. This chapter is
> characterisation from public knowledge,
> kept deliberately brief.

## PrusaSlicer

The ancestor. Prusa Research's slicer,
the direct descendant of Slic3r.
Same slicing lineage as OrcaSlicer —
much of what chapters 2 and 4 teach
transfers unchanged: presets, profiles,
inheritance. Its home turf is Prusa
printers and people who value a
conservative, well-documented tool.
OrcaSlicer's pull over it: the Bambu
ecosystem, multicolor polish,
and a faster-moving interface.

## Bambu Studio

The parent fork — OrcaSlicer began
as a community fork of it.
Bambu's own slicer is the zero-friction
choice for Bambu printers:
accounts, cloud printing, and machine
integration are all closest to home.
OrcaSlicer exists because users wanted
the same engine with broader printer
support, calibration tools up front,
and development in the open.
Files and profiles travel between
the two more easily than either
travels anywhere else.

## SuperSlicer

The other PrusaSlicer descendant,
aimed at experienced users:
more calibration tooling and more
exposed internals, at the cost
of a steeper welcome.
If OrcaSlicer's expert settings
feel like a subset, SuperSlicer
is where the full set lives.

## UltiMaker Cura

The elder of a different line.
Cura's engine and interface grew up
separately from the Slic3r family,
and its profile library leans
toward UltiMaker and the broad
Creality-era installed base.
Many people's first slicer.
Its settings model — profiles,
quality tiers, a marketplace of
plugins — will feel familiar
in shape and foreign in detail.

## The honest summary

Slicers in the same family produce
near-identical files from near-identical
profiles; the differences that matter
are ecosystem fit, multicolor handling,
and which interface you can stand.
If your printer's vendor ships
an OrcaSlicer profile — and more
than sixty do — you lose little by
starting here, and the G-code
proof habits of chapter 6 move
with you to any of them.
