command-line work. This sandbox could not install packages (the apt lock
was held by another tenant), so the lab downloaded the .deb packages for
the missing libraries and extracted them into orcaslicer-labwork/hostlibs
without installing: 50 packages, resolved from the Ubuntu 24.04 indexes.
LD_LIBRARY_PATH then points at hostlibs plus the AppImage private runtime.
